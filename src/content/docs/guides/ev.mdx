EVs are stats that boost specific attributes of a Pokémon such as Attack, Speed, etc. These values will increase your Pokémon's battle performance similar to [IV](/guides/iv)

# How to view EVs

If you run [/info](/commands/info) on your Pokémon, you can see the EVs in the table. Please note that a single EV stat can reach a maximum of 252, and all EV stats combined can not go over 510.

# How to obtain EVs

Whenever a Pokémon defeats another Pokémon, it earns EVs based on which Pokémon got defeated.
