title: Auction create
description: Put your Pokémon on auction
---

Using this command you can list your Pokémon for sale on the auction

# Command Usage

```
/auction create \<pokeID\> [insta] [hours]
```

# Parameters

- `pokeID` (required): The ID of the Pokémon you want to put on the auction
- `insta` (optional): If this amount of credits is reached, do not wait for the duration to end but instantly sell it
- `hours` (optional): the duration (in hours) for the auction (Discord only). On telegram it is fixed at 36 hours

# Examples

- `/auction create -1`: Put your latest Pokémon on the auction
- `/auction create BulbaGoat`: Put tbe Pokémon with nickname BulbaGoat on the auction
- `/auction create 0 hours:48`: Put your selected Pokémon on the auction for 2 days
- `/auction create 2 insta:1000`: Put your second Pokémon on the auction, automatically end the auction when someone bids more than 1,000
		

Example commands:

/auction create -1 22 , 
Puts your latest Pokémon on auction with a minimum bid of 22 credits.

/auction create 0 50 , 
Puts your selected Pokémon on auction with a minimum bid of 50 credits.

The auction allows other players to place bids. After the duration ends, the highest bidder wins the Pokémon.
